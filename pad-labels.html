Teacher pad (hidden)

Cut this square out and glue foil on it. Clip it to ← LEFT. Keep it on your side of the table. Touching it takes back the last step: a stray count, a wrong answer, anything. You have to be grounded too. Run a second wire from another EARTH hole to a strip of foil you can rest a hand on, same as the Chocolate Touch hidden pad. The grey dot marks the corner the wire goes on.

Setting up

  1. Glue a sheet of foil inside each dashed box. Leave a strip of foil sticking past the edge for the clip.
  2. Clip each pad to its Makey Makey hole using the key above. Plug the Makey Makey into the laptop.
  3. Open the binder and press Makey Makey Practice. Click once on the page so it is listening.
  4. Student holds HOLD in one hand and touches pads with the other. Lift off between counts: a hand resting on COUNT counts once.
  5. Laptop keys for you: N new problem, 1–5 level, P prompts on/off, M adding / subtracting / both.
