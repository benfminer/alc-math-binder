Dividing Fractions

There is a trick for this one. You never really divide. You flip the second fraction and multiply instead.

What dividing fractions means

When you see 12÷14 you are asking:

How many quarters fit inside one half? The answer is 2. Half a pizza holds two quarter slices.

First, learn the flip

To flip a fraction, swap the top number and the bottom number. The flipped fraction is called the reciprocal.

29flips to92

A whole number flips too. Just put a 1 under it first.

7 = 71 flips to 17

The steps

Follow along with this problem: 34÷23

  1. Keep the first fraction exactly as it is.

    34÷23

  2. Change the divide sign to a multiply sign.

    34×23

  3. Flip the second fraction. Only the second one.

    34×32

  4. Multiply straight across. Tops together, then bottoms together.

    3 × 3 = 9    4 × 2 = 8

    98

  5. Tidy up. The top is bigger than the bottom, so write it as a mixed number.

    98=118

Step 1 of 5 showing.

Watch out for this

Only the second fraction gets flipped. Learners often flip the first one by mistake. Say it out loud as you work: keep, change, flip.

Try it yourself

Type fractions like this: 9/8. Mixed numbers like this: 1 1/8. Either form is fine.
